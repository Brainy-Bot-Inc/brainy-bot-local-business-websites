# Local Business Websites - Woodburn, Oregon Area

Complete, ready-to-use websites for real local businesses in the Woodburn, Oregon area
that do not currently have a website. Built from actual directory and local research.

## Businesses included (6 verified)

1. [Barkley's Jewelry and Pawn](Barkley's%20Jewelry%20and%20Pawn/) - pawn shop, Woodburn OR
2. [Ross Mobile Mechanic](Ross%20Mobile%20Mechanic/) - mobile auto repair, Woodburn OR
3. [Key Home Improvements](Key%20Home%20Improvements/) - gutter installation, Salem/Woodburn OR
4. [Kings Cupboard](Kings%20Cupboard/) - health food store, Woodburn OR
5. [Sasquatch Services](Sasquatch%20Services/) - tree service/excavation, Blue River OR (within 50 mi)
6. [Trapala Restaurant](Trapala%20Restaurant/) - Mexican restaurant, Woodburn OR

Each business folder contains:
- `index.html` - the complete homepage (about, services, hours, reviews)
- `README.md` - business details and verification notes
- `email.md` - a $500 outreach email draft

## Shared files

- `real-styles.css` - shared stylesheet used by all business sites

## How to edit (no coding required)

1. Open the business's `index.html` in any text editor (Notepad, VS Code, Sublime Text).
2. Find the business name, address, phone, hours, services, and reviews.
3. Replace them with the verified business details.
4. Save the file.

## How to publish (free)

1. The repo is already at `Brainy-Bot-Inc/brainy-bot-local-business-websites` on GitHub.
2. In the repo: Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch" > `main` > Save.
3. Each business site will be live at:
   `https://brainy-bot-inc.github.io/brainy-bot-local-business-websites/<business-folder-name>/`
