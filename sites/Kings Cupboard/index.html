About Kings Cupboard

Health food market - gluten-free, kosher and wholesome staples

Kings Cupboard is a small health food market on N Pacific Highway in Woodburn. We stock gluten-free, kosher and specialty groceries, fresh produce, supplements and everyday staples for shoppers who care about what they buy - with staff who actually know the products.
