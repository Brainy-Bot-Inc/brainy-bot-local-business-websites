## About

Barkley's Jewelry and Pawn has been part of the Woodburn community since 1995. We are a family-run shop that buys, sells and trades jewelry, watches, gold, firearms and everyday gear - and when you need cash quickly we offer fast, friendly collateral loans with renewable terms.

## Services

- **Collateral loans** - Fast cash against jewelry, tools, firearms and more, with renewable terms.
- **Gold buying** - Scrap gold, broken jewelry and dental gold turned into cash the same day.
- **Jewelry & watch repair** - Battery replacement, band sizing and repair on most wrist watches.
- **Rings & fine jewelry** - New and pre-owned engagement rings, sterling silver and gemstones.
- **Watches & timepieces** - Modern and vintage watches, plus in-house battery service.
- **Firearms** - Licensed FFL dealer with new and used firearms, inspected and guaranteed.
- **Instruments & gear** - Used guitars, amplifiers and music gear bought and sold.
- **Estate purchases** - We buy estate items and large collections - we'll even make house calls.
