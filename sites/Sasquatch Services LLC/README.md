## About

Sasquatch Services LLC is a family-owned, licensed and insured contractor based in Blue River, Oregon (CCB #244527). We take on the tough jobs - hazard trees, storm damage, overgrown land - and we are happy to work directly with your insurance company.

## Services

- **Tree removal** - Large and small, including leaning hazard trees near structures.
- **Hazard tree assessment** - Dangerous and storm-damaged trees identified and taken down safely.
- **Limbing & pruning** - Crown lifting, thinning and pruning for health and clearance.
- **View clearance** - Open up a ridge line or river view without killing the tree.
- **Excavation & brush hogging** - Clearing, grading and brush cutting on rough ground.
- **Storm cleanup & dump runs** - Debris hauled off so your property is left clean.
- **Handyman & maintenance** - Year-round property maintenance for rentals and homes.
- **Insurance-friendly billing** - We work directly with your adjuster on covered work.
