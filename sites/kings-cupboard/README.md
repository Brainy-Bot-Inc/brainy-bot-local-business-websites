## About

Kings Cupboard is a small health food market on N Pacific Highway in Woodburn. We stock gluten-free, kosher and specialty groceries, fresh produce, supplements and everyday staples for shoppers who care about what they buy - with staff who actually know the products.

## Services

- **Gluten-free groceries** - Baking mixes, pasta, snacks and staples from trusted brands.
- **Kosher & organic** - Certified kosher and organic pantry items and produce.
- **Fresh produce** - Fruit and vegetables chosen for quality, not just shelf life.
- **Vitamins & supplements** - Everyday supplements, herbs and wellness products.
- **Snacks & pantry** - Bulk snacks, oils, spices and hard-to-find specialties.
- **Gift & greeting cards** - Cards and small gifts for every occasion.
