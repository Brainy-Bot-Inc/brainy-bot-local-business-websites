About Us

Kings Cupboard is a unique health food market located on North Pacific Highway in Woodburn, Oregon. We specialize in gluten-free, kosher, and other healthy food options you won't find at regular grocery stores. Whether you have dietary restrictions or just love exploring new flavors, you'll find something you love here.

Services

  • Kosher products
  • Organic groceries and pantry items
  • Gourmet sauces, toppings, and snacks
