About Us

Trapala Restaurant LLC is a family-owned Mexican restaurant located in downtown Woodburn, Oregon. We serve authentic Mexican cuisine with fresh ingredients and traditional recipes. Whether you're stopping in for lunch, dinner, or a quick bite, you'll find flavorful, home-style Mexican food made with care.
